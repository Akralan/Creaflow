Le compromis est moins confortable pour moi : le modèle et le compteur doivent tenir sur l’appareil utilisé pendant la séance. Mais les images de Julien restent sur son téléphone.

Événement : 2025-12-06 — Le tremblement fait compter trois fois une répétition
TITRE : Three Counts at the Bottom

The knee angle was calculated correctly. A tremor still made one squat count three times.

On December 6, I checked the squat counter after moving the processing onto the phone. The angle uses the dot product of the hip→knee and ankle→knee vectors.

The thresholds were clear: above 160° for the high position, below 100° for the low position.

The problem appeared at the bottom of the movement. When the leg trembled, the angle passed around 100° three times. Each passage triggered the counter.

So the formula gave me the right angle, but the repetition count was still wrong. That distinction matters in a rehabilitation session: the dashboard could show three repetitions when the patient had done one.

I did not correct it on December 6. The failure stayed in the prototype, exactly where the next exercise tests would find it.
