Événement : 2025-11-16 — Le prototype rate une répétition sur trois sur l'iPhone 11
TITRE : The 6 FPS Gap

On my laptop, the squat counter worked. On Julien’s iPhone 11, it ran at 6 fps and missed one repetition out of three.

The prototype was a web page using MediaPipe Pose in JavaScript.

The counter looked simple: it detected a squat when the hip-knee-ankle angle went below 100°.

That rule was good enough on my laptop. It was not good enough on the phone Julien would actually use.

He still did 20 squats in his living room so I could measure the failure.

The issue was not the exercise definition alone. The camera stream was too slow, so the counter did not see enough of the movement to count every repetition.

At 6 fps, one repetition in three disappeared.

That made the prototype unusable for a patient session, even though the same page appeared to work on my laptop.

This was one iPhone 11 and one exercise, not a device benchmark. Before calling the approach reliable, I still needed to measure the count across phones and repetitions.

Événement : 2025-12-06 — Le tremblement fait compter trois fois une répétition
TITRE : The threshold counted the tremor

One tremor at the bottom of a squat became three repetitions.

On December 6, the knee angle itself was correct. I calculate it with the dot product of the hip-to-knee and ankle-to-knee vectors.

The thresholds were simple: above 160° for the high position, below 100° for the low position.

The failure appeared at the lower threshold. When the leg trembles around 100°, the angle crosses that boundary three times. The counter records three repetitions.

The failure is in the counting transition, not in the angle calculation. A measurement can be technically correct and still produce a bad session.

I did not correct it in this version.

At this point, I know the angle calculation and the two thresholds work in a stable case. I do not yet have a reliable count when the patient trembles at the bottom.

The missing reliability check is clear: measure the count on repetitions with tremor, not only on a clean movement.
